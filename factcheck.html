📚 THE SIFT METHOD

Stop. Investigate. Find. Trace.

S
STOP
Before sharing, pause. Emotional reactions are exactly what disinformation is designed to trigger.
I
INVESTIGATE
Check the source before reading the content. Who published this? What is their reputation?
F
FIND
Find better coverage. Look for what other credible outlets are reporting on the same story.
T
TRACE
Trace claims back to their original context. Quotes and images are frequently manipulated.
