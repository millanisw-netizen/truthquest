📋 THE CRAAP TEST

A framework for evaluating any source

C — Currency

When was the source published or last updated? Is the information current enough for your needs? Check dates on statistics and studies.

R — Relevance

Does the source directly relate to your question? Is it written for a general audience or specialists? Is there something better available?

A — Authority

Who created this? What are their credentials? Is the publisher reputable? Can you find information about the author independently?

A — Accuracy

Is the information supported by evidence? Are sources cited? Is the language emotionally neutral? Has it been peer reviewed?

P — Purpose

Why does this source exist? To inform, sell, entertain, or persuade? Is there a clear bias? Is the intention transparent?
