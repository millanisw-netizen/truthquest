🧠 PLATFORM-SPECIFIC TACTICS

How disinformation spreads differently on each platform

📱 TikTok

Algorithmic amplification of emotional content means a misleading video can reach millions before fact-checkers see it. Audio trends allow false narratives to spread via sound alone.

📘 Facebook

Closed groups create echo chambers impervious to correction. The "share" mechanic removes original context. Older users have higher engagement with false health claims.

💬 WhatsApp

End-to-end encryption makes moderation impossible. "Forwarded many times" is the only warning. Social trust — sharing from family/friends — dramatically reduces scepticism.

✈️ Telegram

Large public channels with no moderation. Often used as the original source for coordinated disinformation before it migrates to other platforms. Screenshot chains remove context.

▶️ YouTube

Recommendation algorithm creates "rabbit holes" of increasingly extreme content. Long-form format allows complex conspiracy theories to appear well-researched. Monetisation incentivises outrage.
